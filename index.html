Dance Director & Fine Arts Department Lead, Woodland High School · 25+ years teaching dance from pre-K through university

Tiffany Tindall is a K-12 dance educator and school leader who has spent 25+ years proving that rigorous, standards-based dance belongs to every student. She writes standards from the classroom up: she helped write Georgia’s dance standards, she teaches them daily to students of every ability in one room, and she studies her own practice through national action research.

Georgia Dance Standards Writing Committee (2008) NDEO National Conference Presenter (2025) Connected Arts Network National PLC Teacher of the Year (2007–08)

Connected Arts Network

Since January 2024, Tiffany has served as a Connected Arts Network (CAN) Teacher Participant through NDEO—a federally funded national professional learning community sponsored by NAEA, EdTA, NAfME, NDEO, and NYC Public Schools Arts Office. CAN is a direct bridge to the NCAS review: project manager Janine Campbell is a former CAN PLC Teacher Leader for NAEA.

Her 2024–25 action research asked: How can SEL practices help students with different levels of training succeed so everyone feels valued? She is continuing classroom action research in 2025–26.
