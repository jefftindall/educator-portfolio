# Tiffany Tindall — Personal Brand Style Guide

Oct 4, 2026 · @Jeffrey Tindall

> This is the source of truth for the site's look and voice. How it maps to code is in [In code](#in-code) at the end. The agent guardrails are in [`.cursor/rules/tiffany-brand.mdc`](../../.cursor/rules/tiffany-brand.mdc).

## Brand foundation

The brand should present Tiffany as an arts education leader who happens to teach dance, not a dance teacher who wants a promotion. Every choice below serves that shift from classroom to system-level leadership.

**Positioning statement:** Tiffany Tindall is an award-winning educator and fine arts leader who works to make high-quality arts education available to every student.

**Audiences, in priority order:**

1. District and state decision-makers: superintendents, boards, hiring committees
2. Arts education peers and professional organizations
3. Parents, community partners and funders
4. Students and alumni

**Brand traits.** Each asset should show at least two of these.

| Trait | What it looks like | What it avoids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credible | Results, recognition, clear structure | Vague claims, clutter |
| Warm | Real photos, first-person voice, generous spacing | Corporate stiffness |
| Artful | One expressive typeface, one movement motif | Decoration for its own sake |
| Inclusive | Accessible design, every art form shown, plain language | Dance-only imagery, jargon |

**Independence from the school.** The brand stands apart from Woodland High School and the Dancing Wildcats so it can travel with her to any role. It uses no school colors, mascots or logos.

## Color palette

The palette pairs a deep indigo for authority with a stage-light gold and a calm teal, set on warm neutrals. Indigo reads as trustworthy to administrators; gold and teal keep it recognizably about the arts.

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Midnight Indigo | #1E2A5A | 30, 42, 90 | Headings, navigation, footer, dark sections |
| Secondary | Studio Teal | #1B7F79 | 27, 127, 121 | Links, icons, subheads, secondary buttons |
| Accent | Spotlight Gold | #E8A917 | 232, 169, 23 | Primary buttons, highlights, the arc motif |
| Neutral | Warm Ivory | #FAF6EE | 250, 246, 238 | Page backgrounds |
| Neutral | Soft Stone | #E7E2D8 | 231, 226, 216 | Cards, dividers, table stripes |
| Neutral | Charcoal | #2B2B33 | 43, 43, 51 | Body text |

**Proportions.** Aim for roughly 60% neutrals, 30% indigo, and 10% teal and gold combined. Gold is the scarce color: one gold element per screen draws the eye to the most important action.

**Approved text pairings.** Contrast ratios are approximate and all meet WCAG AA.

| Text | Background | Contrast | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | Warm Ivory | 13:1 | Default body text |
| Midnight Indigo | Warm Ivory | 12.7:1 | Headings |
| White or Ivory | Midnight Indigo | 13:1 | Dark sections, footer |
| Spotlight Gold | Midnight Indigo | 6.6:1 | Highlights on dark |
| Midnight Indigo | Spotlight Gold | 6.6:1 | Button labels |
| Studio Teal | White | 4.8:1 | Links; use at 16px or larger |

**Never:** gold text on white or ivory (about 2:1, unreadable for many people), teal text on indigo, or gradients between the three brand colors.

## Typography

Use two typefaces: Fraunces for headings and Source Sans 3 for everything else. Both are free on Google Fonts, so the site, slides and documents can all match.

- **Fraunces** (serif) has soft, slightly curved letterforms that suggest movement while still reading as established and serious.
- **Source Sans 3** (sans-serif) is plain, highly legible at small sizes, and covers a wide range of languages.

| Style | Typeface | Weight | Web size | Line height |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1, page title | Fraunces | 600 | 48px | 1.1 |
| H2, section | Fraunces | 600 | 32px | 1.2 |
| H3, subsection | Source Sans 3 | 700 | 22px | 1.3 |
| Body | Source Sans 3 | 400 | 18px | 1.6 |
| Pull quote | Fraunces Italic | 400 | 26px | 1.4 |
| Label, caption | Source Sans 3 | 600 | 14px, uppercase, 0.08em spacing | 1.4 |

**Rules:**

- Left-align body text and keep lines to about 70 characters.
- Use uppercase only for short labels, never for sentences.
- Use bold for emphasis; reserve italics for quotes and titles of works.
- On phones, reduce H1 to 34px and H2 to 26px; keep body at 18px.

**Fallbacks.** Where the fonts cannot be installed, such as district email or shared slide decks, use Georgia for headings and Arial for body.

## Wordmark and monogram

Her name is the logo: "Tiffany Tindall" set in Fraunces 600, with a small descriptor line beneath it. A typographic wordmark ages better than a symbol and keeps the focus on her as a person.

**Primary wordmark**

- Line 1: Tiffany Tindall, Fraunces 600, Midnight Indigo
- Line 2: ARTS EDUCATION LEADER, Source Sans 3 600, uppercase, 0.08em spacing, Studio Teal
- A thin Spotlight Gold arc sits under the name, rising left to right

**Monogram.** The initials TT in Fraunces inside a Midnight Indigo circle, with the gold arc beneath. Use it for the favicon, social avatars and slide footers.

**Versions to produce**

| Version | Colors | Use on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full color | Indigo, teal, gold | Ivory or white backgrounds |
| Reversed | Ivory text, gold arc | Indigo backgrounds and photos |
| One color | All indigo or all white | Print, embroidery, small sizes |

**Usage rules**

- Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the capital T.
- Minimum width is 140px on screen and 1.25 inches in print; below that, use the monogram.
- Do not stretch, recolor, add shadows, or place it on a busy photo without a dark overlay.

**Open question:** which degrees or certifications should follow her name in formal contexts, such as M.Ed. or Ed.S.? Add them only on résumés, speaker bios and email signatures.

## Iconography and graphic elements

Use simple line icons and one signature motif, a single sweeping arc. Together they suggest movement and craft without resorting to clip art.

**Icon style**

- Outline icons with a 1.5 to 2px stroke, rounded ends and no fills
- One color per icon: Studio Teal on light backgrounds, Spotlight Gold or Ivory on indigo
- Draw every icon from one open-source set so they match; Lucide and Phosphor both fit this style
- Standard sizes: 24px inline, 40px for feature blocks
- Always pair an icon with a text label

**Represent all the arts.** When icons or images stand for her field, show dance, music, theatre and visual art together. A system-level leader advocates for all four.

**The arc motif.** One continuous curved line, like the path a dancer's hand traces or a conductor's gesture. Use it:

- Under the wordmark
- As a divider between page sections
- As a large, faint shape behind portraits and hero text
- In gold on indigo, or in Soft Stone on ivory when it should recede

**Shapes and surfaces**

- Circles for portraits and the monogram
- Rounded rectangles with an 8px corner radius for cards and buttons
- Flat color and thin dividers; no heavy drop shadows

**Avoid:** ballet-slipper, comedy-and-tragedy-mask and paint-palette clip art, glitter or stage-curtain textures, and emoji used as icons. These read as classroom decor, not leadership.

## Photography and imagery

Photos should show Tiffany leading, not only teaching: speaking, coaching colleagues and directing, alongside time in the studio. Photography will do more for credibility than any other element, so a professional session is worth the cost.

**Shot list for one session**

| Shot | Purpose | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headshot | Site header, LinkedIn, speaker bios | Shoulders up, direct eye contact, plain backdrop |
| Three-quarter portrait | About page, press | Standing, relaxed, in a studio or theatre |
| Leading | Home page, slides | Presenting or facilitating adults |
| Teaching | Work and impact pages | Mid-instruction, students in motion |
| Detail | Backgrounds, dividers | Hands, floor, stage lights, sheet music, brushes |

**Wardrobe.** Solid colors that sit with the palette: navy, ivory, teal or charcoal, with one gold accent at most. Choose a polished blazer for portraits rather than studio wear.

**Treatment**

- Natural color with slightly warm tones; no heavy filters
- For text over photos, add a Midnight Indigo overlay at 60 to 70% opacity
- Crop portraits to circles for avatars and 4:5 for page features

**Students in photos**

- Publish only images covered by the district's media release, and confirm district policy before using school event photos on a personal site.
- Prefer wide shots, groups, silhouettes and motion over identifiable close-ups.
- Do not caption students by name.
- Show a range of students and of art forms, in line with "arts education for all."

**Avoid** stock photos of generic dancers or classrooms. An empty stage she has actually worked on is stronger than a polished stranger.

## Voice and messaging

Write in the first person, in a voice that is confident, warm and specific. Lead with what students and programs gained, then name her role in it.

| Do | Instead of |
| --- | --- |
| "I lead a fine arts department serving students across dance, music, theatre and visual art." | "I'm just a dance teacher who loves her kids." |
| Numbers: enrollment, performances, partnerships, funding | "Passionate," "dedicated," "world-class" |
| "Every student," "access," "the arts as core curriculum" | Education acronyms and insider jargon |
| Short sentences, active verbs | Long mission-statement paragraphs |

**Tagline options**

1. Arts education for all.
2. Every student deserves the arts.
3. Moving arts education forward.

Option 1 is the recommendation: it is her stated cause in four words and works at county, state and national scale.

**Proof points to feature early**

- Woodland High School Teacher of the Year, 2026
- Bartow County School System High School Teacher of the Year
- Fine Arts Department Lead, Woodland High School

**Bio lengths to keep on file**

| Length | Use | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One line | Social profiles, slide footers | Role, cause, top honor |
| 50 words | Conference programs, introductions | Adds scope of leadership and one result |
| 150 words | About page, press, applications | Adds philosophy, results and long-term goal |

**Titles.** In headings use "Arts Education Leader." In bios use her exact current title, "Dance Teacher and Fine Arts Department Lead." Do not use an administrative title she does not yet hold.

## Accessibility

Every asset should meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. A brand built on "arts education for all" loses credibility if some people cannot read or navigate it.

- [ ] Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for text 24px and larger; use only the approved pairings above
- [ ] Body text is 18px on the web and at least 11pt in documents
- [ ] Every meaningful image has alt text that describes the action, not just "dance photo"
- [ ] Videos have captions; performance videos also get a short written description of the movement
- [ ] Color is never the only signal: links are underlined, and charts use labels as well as color
- [ ] Link text says where it goes, never "click here"
- [ ] The site works by keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline
- [ ] Animation is subtle and turns off when a visitor's device asks for reduced motion
- [ ] PDFs are tagged, with real headings and a set reading order
- [ ] Copy reads at roughly an eighth-grade level, so parents and community members are included

## Applying the system

The same six colors, two typefaces and one arc appear on every asset, so each touchpoint is recognizably hers.

| Asset | Key specifications |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Ivory page background, indigo header and footer, one gold button per screen, arc dividers between sections |
| Email signature | Name in bold, title, tagline, site link; monogram at 48px; no quote or animated image |
| LinkedIn | Circular headshot; 1584 × 396px indigo banner with reversed wordmark and tagline; headline matches the one-line bio |
| Slide decks | Indigo title and section slides, ivory content slides, monogram in the footer, one idea per slide |
| Résumé and one-page bio | Fraunces name and section heads, Source Sans 3 body, teal rules, gold used once |
| Social graphics | Three templates: quote on indigo, photo with indigo overlay, announcement on ivory |
| Favicon and avatars | Monogram in the indigo circle |

**Suggested site structure:** Home, About, Leadership and Impact, Speaking and Workshops, Recognition, Contact. Put the two Teacher of the Year honors and a headshot on the first screen.

**Use a personal domain and email address** rather than district accounts, so the brand stays hers through any change of role.

2. Professional photo session

## In code

How the guide is built into the site. Update this section when the system changes.

**Colors.** [`src/styles/global.css`](../../src/styles/global.css) clears Tailwind's default palette (`--color-*: initial`), so only these utilities exist. An off-palette class such as `text-gray-600` simply won't generate.

| Guide name | Utility suffix | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Indigo | `midnight` | `text-midnight`, `bg-midnight` |
| Studio Teal | `studio` | `text-studio`, `border-studio` |
| Spotlight Gold | `spotlight` | `bg-spotlight` (buttons and arcs only, never text on light) |
| Warm Ivory | `ivory` | page background (set on `body`) |
| Soft Stone | `soft-stone` | `border-soft-stone`, table stripes, receding arcs |
| Charcoal | `charcoal` | body text (set on `body`) |
| White | `white` | cards that hold teal text |

**Type.** Fraunces and Source Sans 3 are self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable/*` (imported in `BaseLayout.astro`), so there are no Google Fonts requests. The base layer sets the type scale on `body`, `h1`, `h2`, and `h3`, including the phone sizes. Use plain `<h1>`/`<h2>`/`<h3>` and don't add size classes. Helper utilities:

- `label`: 14px, 600 weight, uppercase, 0.08em letter spacing
- `pull-quote`: Fraunces Italic at 26px

**Surfaces and buttons.**

- `surface-dark`: indigo background with ivory text. Inside it, links turn ivory and the focus ring turns gold, because teal on indigo is banned.
- `btn-primary`: gold button with an indigo label. Use one per screen.
- `btn-secondary`: teal outline button on white.
- `rounded-brand`: the 8px corner radius for cards, buttons, and media. Don't use shadows.

**Components** (in `src/components/`):

| Component | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `Wordmark.astro` | Name, gold arc, and descriptor. Use `variant="reversed"` on indigo. |
| `Arc.astro` / `ArcDivider.astro` | The arc motif: dividers, and faint shapes behind headers |
| `Icon.astro` | Lucide outline paths only. Add new icons from Lucide and always pair them with a text label. |
| `BadgeList.astro` | Honors and proof points, each with an award icon |
| `public/favicon.svg` | Monogram: TT on an indigo circle with the gold arc. The TT is Fraunces 600 converted to outlines, so it doesn't depend on installed fonts. |
| `public/favicon-32.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png` | PNG monogram for browsers and iOS home screens that don't use SVG icons |

The monogram files are generated, not hand-edited. To regenerate them, outline "TT" from `@fontsource/fraunces` (`fraunces-latin-600-normal.woff`) with `opentype.js`, then render the PNGs from the SVG with `sharp`.

**Notes:**

- Studio Teal on Warm Ivory measures about 4.47:1, just under AA's 4.5:1 for small text. Jeff has accepted this (Oct 4, 2026), so teal text on ivory is approved.
- The NDEO conference photo stands in for the headshot until the professional photo session.
